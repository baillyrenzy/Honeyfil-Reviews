Safety First

Read this before you take any performance supplement.

Talk to a professional

See your doctor or pharmacist first if you take prescription medicine, especially for heart, blood pressure or diabetes, or if you have any ongoing health condition.

Stop if something feels wrong

Stop use and get medical help for chest pain, dizziness, a racing heartbeat, rash, swelling or any severe reaction.

Watch for hidden drugs

Regulators have found undeclared drug ingredients in some products sold for male performance. Buy only from official sellers and avoid unknown resellers.

Persistent problems

Ongoing performance or energy concerns can have medical causes. A checkup is the right first step, not a supplement.
